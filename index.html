Aura

A social web app with a real economy. Users post about their day and the community votes on their "aura" score. Votes cost the voter aura to send, negative users get taxed 25% into a weekly prize pool, and the top post wins the pot. Full auth, leaderboards, photo uploads, user tagging with aura splits, streak penalties, and a transaction ledger. Installable as a PWA on iOS and Android.
